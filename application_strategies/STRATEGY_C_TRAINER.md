## Target Context

Teacher training providers, CPD coordinators, curriculum leads, and education quality assurance roles. Recruiters prioritise scale of impact, systems thinking, professional development philosophy, and evidence of measurable teacher improvement.

**Typical Hiring Panels:** Director of Professional Learning, Head of Quality, Academic Director
**Typical Shortlisting Criteria:** Number of educators trained, programme design, research base, leadership scope
**Decision Timeline:** 2–4 months ahead of start date

## Target Context

Established mid-tier international schools with strong EAL demand. Volume-based hiring pipelines. Recruiters prioritise classroom experience, curriculum breadth, and cross-cultural adaptability. These are the primary market for the 2027 job search window.

**Typical Hiring Panels:** Head of Primary, HR Manager, Recruitment Agency
**Typical Shortlisting Criteria:** Years of experience, curriculum familiarity, EAL specialism, references
**Decision Timeline:** 1–3 months ahead of start date

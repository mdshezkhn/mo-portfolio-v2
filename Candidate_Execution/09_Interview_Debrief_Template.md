  - *Immediate reaction:*

* **Evidence requested that was missing from your portfolio:**
* **Claims challenged by the interviewer:**

#### 4. Action Plan for Next Session
* [ ] Action 1: (e.g. Refrain from corporate wording in story X)
* [ ] Action 2: (e.g. Expand parent communication story details)

## 1. Overview
Many premium international schools require a 30-minute teaching demonstration (either live with pupils or simulated with a panel) during the final recruitment stages. This document provides a framework to ensure your teaching under observation demonstrates structural clarity, EAL scaffolding, and assessment validity.

---

## 2. The 30-Minute Demo Lesson Framework

| Time Block | Lesson Phase | Teacher Role | Student Activity | EAL Scaffolding |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **00 – 05 mins** | **The Starter** | Hook interest; establish rules. | Group discussion around a physical object. | Dual-coding: Vocabulary cards paired with visual slides. |
| **05 – 15 mins** | **Active Inquiry** | Modeling task; introducing STEM concepts. | Hands-on physical interaction (e.g. prism refraction). | Strict 8-second wait-time on questions. |
| **15 – 25 mins** | **Collaborative Application** | Circulate, observe, and diagnose. | Peer-group problem solving and task completion. | Bilingual peer modeling; visual check-list grids. |
| **25 – 30 mins** | **Plenary & Wrap-up** | Exit checks; summarize learning. | Individual exit-ticket response (draw/write). | Non-verbal assessment: Diagram labeling. |

## 3. Board Organization Strategy
A messy board indicates a disorganized lesson to observers. Structure the whiteboard beforehand using this visual template:

```text

|  TODAY'S TARGETS (Objectives) |   VOCABULARY BLOCK      |   EXIT TICKETS    |
```

## 4. Observed Differentiation Strategies
Lesson observers want to see *how* you support struggling learners during active phases:
* **Visual Sentence Stems:** Display sentence frames on the board (e.g., *"I observe that the light ray bend when it passes through..."*) for peer discussions.
* **Dual-Coding Instruction:** Every instruction must be spoken and visually represented (written on the board or shown via icons).
* **Tiered Task Sheets:** Provide 3 levels of writing support:
  - *Tier 1 (High Scaffold):* Diagram-labeling + vocabulary bank.
  - *Tier 2 (Medium Scaffold):* Cloze-test paragraph (fill in the blanks).
  - *Tier 3 (No Scaffold):* Open-ended writing prompt.

---

## 5. Formative Assessment Checkpoints
Do not wait until the exit ticket to check if students understand. Use these quick checks under observation:
* **Mini-Whiteboards (5-second check):** Have all students write their answer on individual whiteboards and hold them up on a count of three. This shows you exactly who is struggling.
* **Fist-to-Five Self-Assessment:** Ask students to rate their confidence by holding up fingers (1 = struggling, 5 = confident).
* **Diagnostic Multiple-Choice:** Present a question with four possible answers (A, B, C, D) where each wrong option represents a specific misconception, letting you diagnose *why* a student is confused.

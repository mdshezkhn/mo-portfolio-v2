## 1. Overview
This question bank is designed to prepare you for realistic, multi-tiered interview scenarios. Rather than practicing random prompts, prepare for **four specific, timed interview simulations** that match the priorities of international school decision-makers.

---

## 2. Interview Simulation 1: Head of Primary (45 mins)
* **Focus:** Classroom delivery, EAL progression, differentiation, lesson planning, and formative assessment.
* **Pass Criteria:** Demonstrating immediate classroom credibility, active phonological knowledge, and child-centered teaching methods.

### Simulated Questions:
1. *"Walk me through a typical English or literacy lesson in your primary classroom. How do you start, and how do you measure progress by the end of the hour?"*  
   - *Target Story:* **Story 1 (Eton House Phonics)**
2. *"We have a wide range of English proficiency in our Year 3 classes. How do you differentiate a science lesson so EAL students can learn the concepts without getting lost in the vocabulary?"*  
   - *Target Story:* **Story 4 (STEM Light Refraction)**
3. *"Describe your approach to early reading and phonics. How do you support a student who has fallen behind their peers in word blending?"*  
   - *Target Story:* **Story 1 (Tactile Blending)**
4. *"Can you give an example of how you use formative assessment during a lesson to adjust your teaching pace?"*  
   - *Target Story:* **Story 1 & Story 4**
5. *"Describe a time a lesson did not go as planned. What did you notice, what decision did you make, and how did you adapt?"*  
   - *Target Story:* **Story 4 Reflection**
6. *"How do you handle low-level disruptive behavior during independent student work?"*  
   - *Target Story:* **Story 3 (Scholars Academy)**

## 3. Interview Simulation 2: School Principal (60 mins)
* **Focus:** Leadership fit, professional judgment, teacher collaboration, retention, and school community fit.
* **Pass Criteria:** Demonstrating middle-leadership potential, teacher mentoring skills, maturity, and a commitment to long-term stability.

### Simulated Questions:
1. *"You have a B.Sc. in Physics and have led teacher-training quality programs. Why do you want to be in a primary school classroom, and what is your long-term career plan?"*  
   - *Target Playbook:* **Objection E (Overqualification)**
2. *"Can you describe a time you mentored or coached a colleague who was struggling with their lesson delivery? How did you approach the feedback?"*  
   - *Target Story:* **Story 2 (WhiteHat Jr Coaching)**
3. *"Our school relies on close collaboration between international and bilingual staff. How do you build trust and collaborate with co-teachers during planning?"*  
   - *Target Story:* **Story 2 (Aoxin Moderation)**
4. *"Your CV shows a few transitions over the last decade. What has driven your career moves, and what are you looking for in your next school?"*  
   - *Target Playbook:* **Objection D (Retention)**
5. *"You do not hold QTS from the UK. How does your qualification path prepare you for the demands of our international curriculum?"*  
   - *Target Playbook:* **Objection A (Non-QTS)**
6. *"How do you handle communication with a parent who disagrees with your academic assessment of their child?"*  
   - *Target Story:* **Story 3 (Parent Alignment)**

## 4. Interview Simulation 3: Safeguarding & Inclusion (30 mins)
* **Focus:** Child protection regulations, safer recruitment checks, inclusion, and student wellbeing.
* **Pass Criteria:** Absolute clarity on reporting lines, duty of care, and identifying behavioral/physical markers of concern.

### Simulated Questions:
1. *"What is your understanding of the duty of care an international educator has regarding child protection and safeguarding?"*  
   - *Target Response:* Absolute priority on student safety; direct reporting to the Designated Safeguarding Lead (DSL) without delay.
2. *"If a primary student makes a disclosure to you during a lesson, what are your immediate steps, and what would you write in your report?"*  
   - *Target Response:* Listen without prompting or leading questions; assure safety; document exact student words; report to DSL immediately; do not investigate independently.
3. *"How do you identify behavioral indicators of neglect or emotional distress in a young student?"*  
   - *Target Story:* **Story 3 (Behavior as Communication)**
4. *"How do you ensure your classroom is inclusive for students with special educational needs (SEN) or neurodivergent profiles?"*  
   - *Target Story:* **Story 3 & Story 1 (Tactile/Visual Scaffolding)**

## 5. Interview Simulation 4: Teaching Demo & Lesson Review (30 mins)
* **Focus:** Lesson planning, explanation of pedagogical decisions, and response to observer feedback.
* **Pass Criteria:** Ability to justify planning choices, differentiate language vs. content, and accept feedback reflectively.

### Simulated Questions:
1. *"Here is a lesson plan you submitted for a primary science unit. Why did you choose this specific physical activity over a reading passage?"*  
   - *Target Story:* **Story 4 (STEM Inquiry)**
2. *"How does this lesson sequence ensure that higher-proficiency English speakers are challenged while lower-proficiency speakers are supported?"*  
   - *Target Story:* **Story 4 (Differentiation & Extension)**
3. *"If the lesson observer noted that student talk time was low during the main activity, how would you restructure the lesson next time?"*  
   - *Target Story:* **Story 1 & Story 4 Reflections**
4. *"How do you ensure that your grading standards align with other classes in the same year group?"*  
   - *Target Story:* **Story 2 (Grade 5 Moderation)**

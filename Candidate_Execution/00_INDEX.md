## 2. Core Operational Rule

To prevent candidate drift and documentation bloat:
> **No new module may be added to the Interview Operating System (IOS) unless it directly replaces or measurably improves an existing capability.**

Before creating any future document, evaluate:
1. What specific hiring problem does this solve?
2. Which existing module is insufficient to address this problem?
3. How will I measure whether this new document improved my live interview performance?

## 3. The 4 Performance Gates (August 2026 – August 2027)

* **Gate 1 — Validation (Aug–Oct 2026):** Conduct 10–15 timed mock interviews, record answers, eliminate inconsistencies, and enforce the 2-minute narrative limit.
* **Gate 2 — Evidence Expansion (Oct 2026–Feb 2027):** Complete concrete classroom artifacts (STEM unit, EAL sequence, assessment package, moderation samples, redacted parent emails).
* **Gate 3 — Market Presence (Jan–Jun 2027):** Build recruiter network, update LinkedIn featured media, track profile views, and research target international school requirements.
* **Gate 4 — Recruitment Campaign (Jul–Aug 2027):** Tailored applications, live teaching demonstrations, contract negotiation, and relocation logistics.

## 4. 7-Day Capstone Recruitment Simulation

This simulation replicates the complete international hiring process under realistic time pressure:
* **Day 1: Identity Review** -> LinkedIn and CV alignment audit.
* **Day 2: Principal Screening** -> 45-minute leadership and compliance interview.
* **Day 3: Technical Screening** -> 45-minute Head of Primary classroom teaching interview.
* **Day 4: Demonstration Lesson** -> Simulated delivery of a lesson block from Module 11.
* **Day 5: Crisis Role-Play** -> 20-minute mock parent dispute or co-teacher conflict.
* **Day 6: Contract Review** -> Salary evaluation and relocation checks (Module 08).
* **Day 7: Performance Debrief** -> Log failures, missing evidence, and revise story files.

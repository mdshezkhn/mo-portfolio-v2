This Command Center is your active operational hub during the 2027 recruitment campaign. It combines target school research guidelines with a pipeline tracker to manage applications from initial contact to contract negotiation.

## 2. Weekly Execution Cycle

Follow this structured cycle during active recruitment blocks to maintain a continuous, feedback-driven practice loop:
* **Monday:** *Rehearsal* — Practice one competency story from `03_Interview_Story_Bank.md` (record and time it).
* **Tuesday:** *Teaching Demo* — Rehearse one demonstration lesson framework from `11_Demonstration_Lesson_Library.md`.
* **Wednesday:** *Evidence Gathering* — Produce or refine one physical lesson asset or student rubric (building toward your 12 Gold-standard artifacts).
* **Thursday:** *Market Engagement* — Update LinkedIn featured links, post an educational reflection, or connect with 3 target school leaders.
* **Friday:** *Pressure Testing* — Complete a simulated mock interview using `06_Mock_Interview_Questions.md`.
* **Saturday:** *Evaluation* — Watch recordings of your practice, log weak answers, and fill out the `09_Interview_Debrief_Template.md`.
* **Sunday:** *IOS Maintenance* — Update only the specific story or playbook sections affected by the week's learning. Do not add new documents.

## 4. Target School Research Checklist

Before submitting any application, complete this checklist to tailor your cover letter and interview responses:
* [ ] **Accreditation Status:** Is the school accredited by CIS, COBIS, WASC, or NEASC?
* [ ] **Student Demographics:** What percentage of the student body are language learners (EAL)?
* [ ] **Curriculum Framework:** Do they follow the UK National Curriculum, IB Primary Years Programme (PYP), or a hybrid?
* [ ] **EAL Delivery Model:** Do they use push-in co-teaching, pull-out support, or mainstream immersion?
* [ ] **Professional Learning (CPD):** Does the school support action research or teacher-led study groups?
* [ ] **Visa/Immigration Limits:** Does this country/ministry of education mandate state QTS for licensing, or do B.Ed. and PGCE credentials suffice?

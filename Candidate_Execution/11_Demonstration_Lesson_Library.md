## Lesson 1: Living vs. Non-Living (Grade 2 Science)
* **Target Competencies:** Questioning, vocabulary scaffolds, formative assessment, early years differentiation.
* **Duration:** 30 minutes.

### 1. Lesson Parameters
* **Objective:** Students can classify objects as living or non-living based on basic life processes (breathing, growing, moving).
* **Key Vocabulary:** Living, Non-living, Grow, Breathe, Move.
* **EAL Scaffolds:** Picture cards matching objects with actions; TPR (Total Physical Response) gestures for verbs (Grow = stand tall, Breathe = deep breath).

### 2. Lesson Pacing
* **00 - 05m (Hook):** Present a real potted plant and a plastic toy plant. Ask: *"What is different? How do we know?"* Apply the 8-second wait-time rule.
* **05 - 15m (Inquiry):** Co-construct a visual checklist on the board: *Does it grow? Does it breathe? Does it move?* Introduce the vocabulary cards.
* **15 - 25m (Active Classification):** Group work: Students sort items (a rock, a worm, a balloon, a shell) into two sorting trays labeled *Living* and *Non-living*.
* **25 - 30m (Plenary):** Show a picture of a car. Ask students to write/draw on their exit slip whether it is living or non-living and why. (Misconception check: *A car moves, but does it breathe?*).

## Lesson 2: Understanding Fractions (Grade 4 Mathematics)
* **Target Competencies:** Manipulatives, misconception checks, guided practice, concrete-pictorial-abstract progression.
* **Duration:** 30 minutes.

### 1. Lesson Parameters
* **Objective:** Students can identify and write fractions representing parts of a whole shape.
* **Key Vocabulary:** Fraction, Numerator (part on top), Denominator (whole on bottom), Equal parts.
* **EAL Scaffolds:** Dual-coded fraction cards (numerical fraction paired with color-divided circle drawings).

### 2. Lesson Pacing
* **00 - 05m (Starter):** Draw a square divided into 3 unequal parts and shade 1 part. Ask: *"Is this shaded part one-third?"* (Misconception check: *Parts must be equal*).
* **05 - 15m (Guided Practice):** Hand out physical paper strips. Guide students to fold strips into 2, 4, and 8 equal parts, writing the fraction on each part.
* **15 - 25m (Application):** Students work in pairs with fraction circle tiles to solve fraction shading worksheets, matching numerical values to colored sections.
* **25 - 30m (Plenary):** Mini-whiteboard check: Draw a circle with 3 of 4 parts shaded. Students write the fraction and display it on a count of three.

## Lesson 3: Light & Waves (Grade 5 Science)
* **Target Competencies:** Inquiry, Physics STEM integration, EAL scaffolds, diagrammatic assessment.
* **Duration:** 30 minutes.

### 1. Lesson Parameters
* **Objective:** Students can observe and diagram how light travels in a straight line and bends (refracts) when passing through water.
* **Key Vocabulary:** Light ray, Refraction, Straight, Bend, Medium.
* **EAL Scaffolds:** Bilingual labeled diagram sheets, visual ray arrows.

### 2. Lesson Pacing
* **00 - 05m (Hook):** Place a pencil in a half-filled glass of water. Observers view it from the side. Ask: *"Why does the pencil look broken?"*
* **05 - 15m (Inquiry Lab):** Pass out laser pointers/flashlights and water prisms. Students shine the light beam through the prism and observe the change in direction.
* **15 - 25m (Diagramming):** In peer groups, students complete a large diagram tracing the light ray's path, labeling *Air, Water, Incident Ray, and Refracted Ray* using visual labels.
* **25 - 30m (Plenary):** Exit ticket: Label a blank diagram showing the light beam passing from air to glass, drawing arrows to show the path direction.

## Lesson 4: Narrative Writing (Grade 3 English)
* **Target Competencies:** Modeling, collaborative writing, success criteria, talk before writing.
* **Duration:** 30 minutes.

### 1. Lesson Parameters
* **Objective:** Students can write a cohesive opening paragraph for a narrative using sensory details.
* **Key Vocabulary:** Setting, Character, Sensory (Hear, See, Feel).
* **EAL Scaffolds:** Visual sentence starters; sensory word wall with associated icons (Eye = See, Ear = Hear).

### 2. Lesson Pacing
* **00 - 05m (Starter):** Display a dramatic picture of a quiet forest. Ask partners to whisper 3 things they would *hear* in this forest (Talk-before-writing).
* **05 - 15m (Teacher Modeling):** Model writing a story opening on the board, thinking aloud: *"The forest was quiet. I want to make it sensory. The dry leaves crunched under my boots."*
* **15 - 25m (Collaborative Writing):** Students work in pairs to write a 3-sentence opening using a sensory checklist: *[ ] 1 Sight, [ ] 1 Sound, [ ] 1 Action verb.*
* **25 - 30m (Plenary):** Share and review: Peer groups read their openings, and the class checks if the sensory criteria were met.

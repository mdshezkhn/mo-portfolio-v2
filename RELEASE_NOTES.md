## Versioning Policy

We use Semantic Versioning (SemVer) for the portfolio repository governance.

* **Major (X.0.0):** Breaking changes to the canonical schema or major shifts in brand positioning. Example: Redesigning the `CANONICAL_PROFILE.md` data structure.
* **Minor (0.X.0):** Additions of new verified evidence, new roles, or major claim verifications. Example: Updating an employment status from "Pending" to "Verified" upon evidence receipt.
* **Patch (0.0.X):** Typo fixes, minor rewordings in downstream artifacts, or formatting changes that do not alter factual claims. Example: Fixing a date formatting inconsistency.

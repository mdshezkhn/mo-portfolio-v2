## About
Primary EAL educator with 11+ years in education across India and China, combining classroom expertise with instructional quality leadership.

**Career Arc:** Classroom Educator → Teacher Trainer → Instructional Quality Leader → International Primary EAL Specialist → Future EAL/Curriculum Leadership

My journey began with foundational teaching at Scholars Academy (India) and early immersion EAL at Eton House Kindergarten (China), which shaped my approach to language-focused early years education. After progressing into teacher development roles at WhiteHat Jr/BYJU'S and GEDU Global Education—where I led quality assurance across UK, Dubai, and Malta—I deliberately returned to international primary classrooms at Aoxin International School to apply those leadership frameworks directly to multilingual learners.

Today, I specialize in rapidly improving academic English outcomes for multilingual cohorts by integrating explicit language instruction into inquiry-based science and mathematics. My work spans classroom teaching, educator mentoring, curriculum development, and cross-curricular learning designed to strengthen language acquisition.

My career is driven by a simple goal: ensuring non-native speakers can access core academic subjects without falling behind. From designing foundational phonics environments in the early years, to leading Grade 5 writing moderation, I focus on data-driven benchmarks that improve pupil outcomes.

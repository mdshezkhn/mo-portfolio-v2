## 5. Questions to Ask Them
*Never ask logistical questions (salary, holidays) in the first round. Ask pedagogical or strategic questions.*
1. "Given the high percentage of EAL learners in your primary phase, how is language acquisition currently supported during inquiry-based units?"
2. "I noticed in your recent CIS report a focus on standardizing assessment. What is the current moderation process for primary writing?"
3. "What does a successful first year look like for the educator stepping into this specific role?"

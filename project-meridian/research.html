Action Research Project

"A Critical Reflection on How Questioning Strategies Can Be Used to Facilitate High-Quality Teaching and Learning in a Bilingual Primary School Setting."

This practitioner research examined the micro-interactions of the primary bilingual classroom. By recording, coding, and analysing questioning patterns, the study identified specific strategies that support participation equity. The findings provide a framework for educators to move beyond closed, recall-based questions and facilitate genuine dialogic teaching without overwhelming the linguistic capacity of EAL learners.

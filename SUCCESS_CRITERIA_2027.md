# Success Criteria — August 2027 Recruitment Campaign

> **Operational Directive**: This document defines career outcome metrics for the August 2027 international-school recruitment campaign. The cognitive overhead of maintaining factual consistency across recruiter-facing assets is substantially reduced; professional execution is active.

## Campaign Success Indicators

| Objective | Success Indicator | Target / Benchmark | Frequency |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Applications Submitted** | Tailored application packages released | Target number of Tier-1/2 school applications | Monthly per cycle |
| **Evidence Quality** | Proportion of published factual assertions backed by V5 primary documents | **90%+ V5 Primary** (100% V4+ Corroborated) | Monthly review |
| **Interview Readiness** | Prepared evidence-backed STAR stories across core competencies (Inquiry, EAL, Assessment) | 100% of core competencies backed by V5/V4 evidence | Pre-interview |
| **Portfolio Quality** | Recruiter assets generated from canonical models & passing policy gates | `PASS` Build Status on all released packages | Per release |
| **Continuous Improvement** | Ingestion of new teaching artifacts, observations, and student outcomes | Monthly evidence ingestion & audit log check | Monthly |

## 5 Monthly Career Progress Questions

Every month, review the repository against these five questions:

1. **Did I add meaningful new evidence this month?**
2. **Did any recruiter-facing facts become outdated?**
3. **Can I answer more interview questions with documented evidence than I could last month?**
4. **Has my portfolio become more persuasive because of new classroom work?**
5. **If I applied tomorrow, would I be more competitive than I was 30 days ago?**

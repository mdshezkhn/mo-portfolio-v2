## Analytics (Update Monthly)

### What Generates Interviews
- Tailored CVs matching job description keywords
- Cover letters with specific school research
- Applications submitted within 3 days of posting
- Leveraging alumni or professional network connections

### What Doesn't Work
- Generic CV_Master for specialized roles
- Cover letters without school-specific references
- Applications submitted after 2 weeks of posting
- Overly verbose bullet points (>2 lines per bullet)

## STAR Story: Changing Practice Based on Evidence
**Situation**: In my bilingual classroom, I noticed that open-ended questions were often answered by the same English-confident students.
**Task**: I wanted to understand if this was a school-wide pattern and how teachers navigated it, so I designed a mixed-methods practitioner study.
**Action**: I collected questionnaire and interview data from 7 colleagues, using a thematic and interpretive coding process to analyze their responses.
**Result**: The data revealed a structural belief-practice gap. Personally, this shifted my own practice: when a student does not answer, I now consciously pause and ask myself whether they might be thinking in Chinese before I assume they do not understand the concept.

## Calculation Method

All means calculated as arithmetic average of the 7 teacher responses:
```
Mean = (Sum of all 7 scores) ÷ 7
```

Example for Q4f (whole-class questioning):
- Scores: 5, 5, 5, 5, 5, 5, 3
- Sum = 33
- Mean = 33 ÷ 7 = 4.714... ≈ 4.7

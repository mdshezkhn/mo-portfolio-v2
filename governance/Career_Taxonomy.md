## 12-Field Taxonomy

| Term | Definition | Canonical Example | Prohibited Synonyms |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Institution** | Accredited educational body (university, college, or equivalent awarding authority) that issues formal qualifications | University of Cumbria | School, Organisation, Provider |
| **Employer** | Legal entity that issued a contract of employment, or the named organisation on a payslip or official letter | Aoxin International School | Company, Client, Placement Agency |
| **Campus** | Physical or digital site of employment within an employer where duties were principally performed | Aoxin Wenzhou Campus | Site, Branch, Location |
| **Department** | Internal division within an employer to which the employee reported or within which they operated | Primary Department | Team, Unit, Faculty (unless the employer uses Faculty formally) |
| **Role** | The aggregate of functional responsibilities held during a position | Primary Educator & Curriculum Lead | Job, Post |
| **Position** | Formally titled appointment within a role as it appears on the employment contract or offer letter | Primary Class Teacher | Job Title (permitted informally), Designation |
| **Employment Type** | Legal or contractual classification of the engagement | Full-time / Part-time / Contract / Visiting / Secondment | Freelance (unless self-employment), Casual |
| **Curriculum** | Educational framework or programme that was designed, delivered, or overseen | British National Curriculum, IB PYP, CBSE | Syllabus (permitted at subject level only), Programme |
| **Age Phase** | Age range of the primary learner group taught or supported | Early Years (0–5), KS1 (5–7), KS2 (7–11), Lower Secondary (11–14) | Key Stage (use only if referring to the statutory UK phase), Year Group |
| **Country** | Country in which the employer is legally registered or where duties were physically performed | China, India | Region, Market |
| **City** | City or municipality in which the Campus is located | Wenzhou, Hangzhou, Bangalore | Province (do not substitute for City) |
| **Placement** | A time-limited role without a permanent contract, typically supervised by a third party or host institution | PGCE Teaching Practice Placement | Practicum, Internship (unless formally titled as such) |

## Application to Career Records

### Employment Entries

Each employment entry must specify:
- **Employer** (not "Organisation" or "Company")
- **Position** (exact contract title if known; Portfolio Display Title if not)
- **Role** (functional summary — may differ from Position)
- **Employment Type**
- **Campus** (if employer has multiple sites)
- **Department** (if known)
- **Curriculum** (primary curriculum delivered)
- **Age Phase** (primary learner group)
- **Country** and **City**

### Education Entries

Each education entry must specify:
- **Institution** (awarding body)
- **Qualification** (full formal name of the award)
- **Study Period** (dates; format: MMM YYYY – MMM YYYY)

The Professional Story

My teaching journey began in the classrooms of Mumbai and evolved in the international schools of Zhengzhou, China. Across these diverse settings, I have had the privilege of working with multilingual learners, guiding them toward confident academic expression.

With a background in Physics (B.Sc.) alongside deep pedagogical training, I integrate cross-curricular STEM with primary literacy. This approach provides a rich, inquiry-based experience that connects language acquisition to tangible, real-world phenomena.

Beyond the classroom, my career evolved into instructional leadership. I have managed instructional quality for distributed teams of over 200 educators and led professional development for multi-campus organisations across the UK, Dubai, and Malta.

To ensure my practice remains grounded, I pursued a Master's-level PGCE, researching how questioning strategies facilitate high-quality teaching in bilingual settings.

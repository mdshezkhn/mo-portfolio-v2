Systemic Improvement

Leadership in education is about elevating the standard of practice across entire cohorts. During my tenure at WhiteHat Jr and GEDU Global Education, I transitioned from the micro-environment of the classroom to the macro-environment of institutional quality.

Whether managing instructional quality for a distributed team of 200+ educators or designing training frameworks for multi-campus organisations, my goal has consistently been to replace anecdotal feedback with data-driven pedagogical insights.

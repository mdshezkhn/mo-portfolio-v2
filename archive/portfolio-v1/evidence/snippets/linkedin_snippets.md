## Featured Project Summary
**Practitioner Research: Questioning Strategies in Bilingual Settings**
I recently completed a mixed-methods study investigating how open-ended questioning can unintentionally act as a participation filter for EAL learners. By triangulating questionnaire and interview data, the study highlighted the risk of conflating a student's language production ability with their actual conceptual understanding.

## Digital Portfolio Case Study Excerpt
**The Research Problem**
I noticed a pattern where open-ended questions were consistently answered by the same small group of English-confident pupils. This study sought to understand why, and revealed that without an institutional framework, questioning strategies can inadvertently widen participation gaps.

**Key Finding**
3 out of 7 surveyed teachers identified instances where pupils demonstrated conceptual understanding in Chinese but could not articulate it in English. This reinforced my commitment to pausing during student silence to allow for dual-language cognitive processing.

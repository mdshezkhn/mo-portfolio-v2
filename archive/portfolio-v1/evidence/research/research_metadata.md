## Research Purpose

To examine questioning strategies in a bilingual primary school and understand the meanings teachers attach to them (interpretivist aim, not to measure frequency).

## Educational Context

- **School Type**: One bilingual primary school, English–Chinese medium of instruction
- **Grade Range**: Grade 1–6 (teacher participants: C/E/F teach Grade 5–6; A/D teach Grade 1–2; B/G teach Grade 3–4)
- **Country**: China | [REASONABLE INFERENCE]

## Participants

- **Total**: 7 teachers completed questionnaire; 2 of those 7 (Teacher A, Teacher B) also completed semi-structured interviews
- **Sampling**: Convenience/volunteer sample of colleagues at Mo's own school, all with ≥1 full academic year teaching across both English-medium and Chinese-medium lessons

## Duration

Approximately 5-week active research window:
- Week 1: Questionnaire distribution/return
- Weeks 2–3: Semi-structured interviews (Teacher A, Teacher B)
- Weeks 4–5: Two-pass thematic analysis

## Research Design

### Type
Small-scale, mixed-methods practitioner research.

### Paradigm
Interpretivist (Mertens, 2019; Cohen et al., 2018); constructivist orientation to data collection/analysis (Opie & Brown, 2019).

### Methods
Cross-sectional mixed-methods: Likert + open-ended questionnaire (n=7) triangulated with semi-structured interviews (n=2) and two-pass thematic coding.

### Research Questions

1. What gap, if any, exists between teachers' stated beliefs about questioning and their reported classroom practice?
2. Does open-ended questioning work equitably in a bilingual primary classroom?
3. How does English-medium questioning shape which learners appear to understand and which do not?

## Ethics

BERA (2018) guidelines followed:
- Institutional approval secured before participant contact
- Written consent obtained
- Participation information sheet covering right to withdraw
- Sealed-envelope questionnaire return to reduce social-desirability bias
- No audio recording
- Password-protected storage
- Teacher codes A–G used
- Raw data destroyed 6 months post-publication

## Limitations

1. Self-report only (no classroom observation — not approved by school principal)
2. Only 2 of 7 participants interviewed
3. Single school
4. Small sample (n=7)
5. Not statistically generalisable
6. Insider-researcher position

## Success Criteria

Not applicable — this is an interpretivist inquiry, not a criterion-referenced evaluation.

## Statistical Approach

No inferential statistics applied. Essay explicitly states: "Seven participants are too small a sample for inferential statistics; the numbers provide context for the qualitative data; they do not replace it." Likert data used descriptively only.

## Reliability Protocol

Two-pass thematic coding (Braun & Clarke, 2006):
- Pass 1: Open coding for surface patterns
- Pass 2: Interpretive pass for higher-order analytical categories
- Theme retention rule: Evidence must appear in ≥2 independent sources (Cohen et al., 2018)

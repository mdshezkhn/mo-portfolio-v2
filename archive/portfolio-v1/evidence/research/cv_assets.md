## Evidence-First Professional Summary

**PGCE7002 Practitioner Research — Quality Teaching in Educational Settings**

Completed a small-scale mixed-methods practitioner inquiry investigating questioning strategies in a bilingual English–Chinese primary school. Research identified that 5 of 7 teachers showed a gap between their espoused beliefs about open-ended questioning and their reported classroom practice, tracing this to structural factors (class size, pacing pressure, absent policy) rather than knowledge gaps. Critically evaluated Alexander's (2020) dialogic teaching framework, finding it under-accounted for linguistically uneven classrooms. Applied Cummins's BICS–CALP distinction to surface assessment risks when English-medium questioning conflates language production with conceptual understanding. No teacher referenced any shared institutional questioning framework. Master's-level pass (75/100), University of Cumbria.

Practitioner Research

PGCE Action Research Level B

A Critical Reflection on How Questioning Strategies Can Be Used to Facilitate High-Quality Teaching and Learning in a Bilingual Primary School Setting

  • Grade: 75/100 (Master's Level)
  • University of Cumbria · PGCE7002
  • Triangulated Study: 7 Teachers

Investigated questioning methods in a bilingual classroom [C-007]. The study identified that open-ended questions can act as participation filters, favoring high-proficiency speakers.

It reframes EAL support by distinguishing social fluency (BICS) from academic language (CALP), proving the necessity of structured pause times for bilingual cognitive processing.
